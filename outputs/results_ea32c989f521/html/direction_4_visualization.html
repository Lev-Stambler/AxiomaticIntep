Feature Explanation

This feature fires on tokens that begin key semantic chunks in formal or structured text—e.g. the leading word in an instruction (“the” in “Remove the …”), the head of a license or contributor name (“Perm” in “Permissive License,” “Dre” in “Drepper”), the initial segment of an identifier or XML attribute (“To” in “fileID ToRecycleName,” “Are” in “SafeAreas,” “M” in “MPL”), or the first token of a named entity or domain (“P” in “CM Punk,” “.” in “glassfish.dev”). In short, it detects chunk-initial markers—starts of commands, names, license clauses or code/metadata fields—in technical and legal boilerplate.
