Feature Explanation

This neuron is basically a “section‐header/boundary” detector. It lights up on the little tokens that introduce or end titles, headlines or section labels—month abbreviations at the top of news stories (Nov.), team names in all caps (MAN-CHE-STER), category or “News” labels, code‐file markers (pragma once, require …), and the blank‐line or period that separates that header from the body. In short, it marks the structural boundary between a heading and the start of the main text.
