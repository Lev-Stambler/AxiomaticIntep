Feature Explanation

This head turns on whenever the model hits a boundary in a coordinated or parenthetical list—i.e. the conjunctions (and, or, also) that link items and the last word of each item (often an acronym, test‐name or proper noun) just before a comma or other padding. It’s essentially flagging “list/appositive boundaries” in formal or scientific prose.
