Feature Explanation

Based on the examples, this feature seems to represent abbreviations followed by a period. The feature activates strongly on tokens like: H.A.R.T. G.R. O.H.P. R. U.S. Inc. H.E.S. H.M.S. R.E.A.L. Mk. In each case, the period following the abbreviation has the highest activation. The model predicts the period with high probability when this feature is active, but removes or greatly reduces that probability when the feature is ablated. So in summary, the abstract concept captured by this feature appears to be "abbreviation followed by period", where the period punctuation specifically is the key signal the feature is detecting, in the context of a preceding abbreviation.
