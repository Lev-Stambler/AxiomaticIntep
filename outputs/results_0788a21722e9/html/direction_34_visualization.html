Feature Explanation

This neuron fires on the very first “content” word following an opening indefinite article (“A”/“An”)—whether that word is an adjective (hybrid, defining, quantitative), a numeral in an age–year construction (56-, 78-, 20-, 15-, 60-year-old), a study or object noun (Bill), or other head-noun/subword in “A …” noun phrases. In effect it marks the core descriptor that kicks off titles, abstracts or case-report intros.
