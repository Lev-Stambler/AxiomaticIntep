Feature Explanation

This feature detects the “title‐style” tokens you find in Wikipedia headings and article titles—i.e. it fires on the initial fragments of proper names (Or- in Orbison/Orville/Orinoco, Ton- in Tonio, Char- in Charlemagne, Lind- in Lindström, Ast- in Astana, Call- in Call of Duty, Ever- in Everton, Col- in Colossians, Ol- in Olita, de in Spanish names, etc.) as well as the small function words that link them (The, of, or, de). In short, it marks the beginnings and connecting words of title-cased named entities and headings.
