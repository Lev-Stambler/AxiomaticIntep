Feature Explanation

This neuron fires on the little “type‐label” word inside multi-word names or titles—the adjectival or classifier token that identifies the kind of entity or category. In “Munster Senior Hurling Championship” it lights up on Senior, in Beijing Renhe F.C. on Renhe, in Manchester United on United, in Lake Wildwood on Wild, in Blumenort Evangelical Mennonite on Evangelical, in “Louisiana Coastal Communities” on Coastal, in “Horizon League” on Horizon, in “Analysis of prognostic factors” on prognostic, and so on. It zeroes in on that middle word that tells you what class of thing you’re talking about (team, place, church, league, study, etc.).
