Feature Explanation

It detects when the model is “in” a formal, citation‐style proper name—that is, it marks the internal parts (often split in unusual ways) of legal or official entity names, so that tokens like “PRE”, “IC”, “ME” (and their analogues in other writing systems) appear when the text is a legal citation, court name, or similarly formal document label. This feature therefore signals that the context is one of structured, multi‐token proper names (often legal/official) and adjusts tokenization and prediction accordingly.
