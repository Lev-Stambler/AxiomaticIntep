Feature Explanation

This feature is a “lede-boundary” detector: it peaks on the punctuation ending a dateline or header (e.g. the period in “Gov.”, “N.B.”, “Sept.”, “M. Ed.”, “Part 2.”, etc.) and then on the very first content word of the ensuing main text—whether that’s a person’s name (“Connie,” “Brian,” “Marcus”), a group or title head (“Members,” “Chief,” “Capital,” “component,” “Citizens,” “Bisotun”), or other topic-introducing noun. In effect it marks the transition from boilerplate metadata into the article’s lede or section head.
