Feature Explanation

This feature lights up on those tiny subwords or single-token “qualifiers” that carry semantic force at the ends (or on their own) of identifiers and literals—things like unit or dimension suffixes (m in p.m., d in bg3d), numeric indices or version tags (40, 17, e11), boolean flags (false), axis labels (y), annotation keywords (pragma), etc. It’s essentially a detector for the short, often one- or two-subword pieces that act as semantic modifiers or flags in code and technical writing.
