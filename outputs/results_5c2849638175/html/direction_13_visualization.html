Feature Explanation

This neuron seems to fire on structural‐metadata markers – the little tokens that introduce or delimit sections or notes. In Japanese news copy it lights up on the first character of header labels like 更新 (“update”) or 特記 (“special note”), in code comments on the /**…*/ delimiters or the final period after “Authors.” in license blocks, and even on emphasis markers in markdown. In short, it’s looking for section-heading or metadata-boundary tokens.
