Feature Explanation

It appears this feature is sensitive to tokens that serve as “boundary markers” in formally structured text. In many examples a punctuation token (almost always a period) following numbers, abbreviations or proper noun segments—and often coming at the end of a title, date, or identifier—triggers high activation. In short, the feature appears to detect and signal the presence of a formal, structured “end‐of‐segment” marker (a punctuation boundary) that demarcates metadata or citation–like elements in the text.
