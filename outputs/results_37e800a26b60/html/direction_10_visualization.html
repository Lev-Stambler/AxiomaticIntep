Feature Explanation

This feature is basically a sub-token “AR” detector. It lights up whenever the letters “ar” (or closely related fragments like “der,” “Far,” “al,” etc.) appear as a standalone token or subword—very common in names (van der Merwe, McFarland, Margarita, Mont(al)ban, van der Waals, etc.)—and even on the slash “/” when it’s being used as a tight separator (as in “indoor/outdoor” or “A/C”).
