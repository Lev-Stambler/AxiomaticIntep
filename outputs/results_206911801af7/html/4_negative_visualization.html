Feature Explanation

This feature lights up on tokens that occur almost exclusively in the header or title line of scientific‐paper abstracts—especially the big, capitalized “topic” words that kick off a title (Genetic, Molecular, Thermo, Hydro, etc.), occasional units (km), and the closing period. In other words, it’s a “title‐boundary” detector tuned to the style of academic research titles.
