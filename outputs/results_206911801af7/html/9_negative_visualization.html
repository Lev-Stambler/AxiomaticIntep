Feature Explanation

This feature fires on “front‐of‐text” or framing tokens—the little labels and high-salience words that kick off a post, article or section. It lights up on proper names used as topic headings (Australia, Berkeley, NL Central), source or media tags (Reuters Photo, Video:), greetings or discourse markers (Hey), and on degree-intensifiers that set tone (highly, randomized, developer). In short, it flags the tokens that introduce or frame what follows.
