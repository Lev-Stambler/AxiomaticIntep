Feature Explanation

This neuron is a “discourse‐marker cue” detector. It lights up on the function word (often a preposition or negation) that heads a little embedded clause or phrase—things like the “in” of “bear in mind” or “in spite of,” the “matter”/“how” of “no matter how,” the “of” in “part of,” or the “don’t”/“doesn’t” introducing an exception or concession. In other words, it spots the lexical pivot that signals a subordinate, contrastive/conditional, or exception clause.
