Feature Explanation

This feature fires on the little “chunks” that kick off or connect structured identifiers in scientific texts—things like the leading digits or letters in citation keys, section/figure labels, journal abbreviations, organism epithets (e.g. th- in thuringiensis, typh- in typhimurium), acronyms (U in UTI, Th in Th2), and the hyphens or dots that glue those pieces together. It thus marks the boundaries and prefixes of metadata tokens (refs, volumes/pages, species names, etc.) in academic papers.
