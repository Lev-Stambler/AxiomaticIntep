Feature Explanation

This neuron is essentially flagging “metadata” tokens—i.e. the little labels and indices you see in headers, timestamps, filenames or section titles. It lights up on month-names and day/​year numbers in dates (February, maj, 6, 03, 2016…), numeric IDs in file names or lists (393, 2, Item 24…), and single‐word field or section labels (to in “from…to…,” science division, professional boundary, green valley, escape rooms, Groom, β, etc.). In short, it marks out the bits of text that serve as structured‐data tags or field headings.
