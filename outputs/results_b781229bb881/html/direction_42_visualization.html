Feature Explanation

This feature appears to detect when a period follows a capital‐letter token that is part of a name or other abbreviated proper noun. In that context it signals that the period is not terminating a sentence but is instead binding a middle initial or abbreviation to a proper name. This pushes the next token prediction toward a continuation of a name (e.g. “J…”) rather than starting a new sentence (e.g. “He”).
