Feature Explanation

It appears that this feature “fires” on periods used as sentence‐end markers in contexts where a decisively “ended” thought is signaled. In practical terms, it prevents the model from defaulting to a common domain suffix (“.com”) when a sentence really should stop rather than continue as a website. This feature thus encodes the distinction between a true sentence‐end period and a period that might otherwise trigger URL‐like continuations.
