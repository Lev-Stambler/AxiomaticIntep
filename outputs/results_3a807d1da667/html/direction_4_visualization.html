Feature Explanation

This feature fires on “weird” contiguous character chunks—low-frequency n-grams or pure‐whitespace runs that don’t look like ordinary words. You see it light up on odd bigrams/trigrams inside names (e.g. “dn” in a handle, “HU” in McHUGH, “Szech” in Szechuan, “reng” in Derengoski, “ff” in Steffy), on repeats in DNA (“AA”), and even on pure-space tokens (indents). In other words, it picks out rare or non-dictionary substrings (including multi-space runs) within otherwise normal text.
