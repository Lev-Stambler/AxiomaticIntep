Feature Explanation

This feature is a detector for those tight consonant-cluster subword fragments that almost never stand alone in English – the “ub,” “rb,” “idd,” “pid,” “und,” “ird,” “kl,” “ug,” “utt” etc. sequences you see buried inside names (Eubanks, Ayub, Urbana, Eugene), legal citations, technical terms (lepidoptera), and the like. In other words, it lights up on interior C–C (or C–V–C) clusters that mark multi-syllabic roots or rare proper-name/term substrings.
