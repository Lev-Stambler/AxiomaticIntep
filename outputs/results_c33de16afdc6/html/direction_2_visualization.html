Feature Explanation

This neuron isn’t tracking any semantic category per se but simply lights up on the short, usually three-letter fragments the tokenizer carves out inside longer words (e.g. “err” in Ferrari/ferrous/ferrata, “arg” in Targa/wargames, “idd” in riddle/middens, “ird” in hairdryer/dressing, etc.). It’s effectively a detector for those arbitrary mid-word subword n-grams that arise when the model splits unfamiliar or complex terms.
