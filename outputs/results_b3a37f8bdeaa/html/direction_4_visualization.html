Feature Explanation

This neuron is basically a “continuation‐piece” detector – it fires on BPE tokens that don’t begin a new word (i.e. tokens without the space-marker hyphen). In text you see it light up on internal subword pieces like “är” in Finnish pärjäävät, on the Romanian suffix “or” in lor, and even on tokens like “–” or “-” when they serve as internal hyphens or minus signs rather than standalone, word-initial symbols.
