Feature Explanation

This neuron fires whenever the model sees a non‐initial “big” BPE piece—i.e. a long, multi‐character token that continues a word (or is a long repetition of the same character, as in a comment‐separator). In practice it lights up on the suffix/fragments of proper names and compounds (the “ather” in WEATHER, “alli” in palliative, “izu” in Meizu, “ña” in Braña, etc.) or on long runs of punctuation (////////////////////////////////).
