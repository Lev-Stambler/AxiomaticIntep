Feature Explanation

This neuron detects sentence-initial discourse markers—subordinating conjunctions or connective words (For, While, Although, Its, etc.) that open a clause to introduce background, reason, contrast or elaboration. It flags the “hook” at the start of a sentence that signals an explanatory or concessive relationship.
