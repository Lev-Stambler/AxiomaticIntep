Feature Explanation

It appears that this feature “lights up” on the beginning fragments of proper‐noun tokens (often names, places, or branded entities) that are split from their “continuation” suffix. In other words, it signals that the current token is only a prefix (for example “Den” in “Denver”, “Denise” or “Tenterden”, “Fel” in “Felicia”, “Fed” in “Fedora”, “Ama” in “Amazing”, etc.) so that the model will complete the compound name appropriately. Removing the feature makes the model lose that context and default to a generic continuation (“themselves”), confirming its role in bundling together these split subwords into the intended proper name or term.
