Feature Explanation

This feature detects when a token is actually a sub‐portion of a split word—the core stem before an affix (or suffix) is attached. In other words, it activates on the “base” part of a word that has been segmented into pieces (with the remainder, often an ending like “ors,” “akers,” “utor,” etc., left over); it thereby signals the presence of a morphological boundary in a compound or inflected word.
