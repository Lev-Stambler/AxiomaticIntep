Feature Explanation

This neuron seems to light up whenever the text is signaling a link or boundary between two pieces—i.e. the “join-point” tokens that relate or separate subexpressions. In math that’s conjunctions (“and”), quantifiers (“∀”), commas before “for all,” equality signs in joins; in prose it’s pronouns (“it”) referring back; in code it’s the property names or delimiters (`roleArn`, `authorizerDescription`, the `]);`/`});`/`)));` that close a call). In every case it marks the connective or closing delimiter that ties two entities or clauses together.
