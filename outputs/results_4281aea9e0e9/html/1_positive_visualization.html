Feature Explanation

This neuron is basically picking out “atomic” one‐ or two‐character content tokens—things like single CJK characters used as counters or classifiers (冊, 回, 文, 可, 矩, etc.) and single Latin letters or very short letter sequences at the start of acronyms, names or domains (G in GIST, H in Hounds, m in m\.example, F in Finder, W in Waves, ins in Insider, beta in math, v in vents, M in MIA…). In other words, it flags standalone minimal lexical units rather than longer function words or affixes.
