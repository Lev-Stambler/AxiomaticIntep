Feature Explanation

This feature seems to fire on the little “linking” words that introduce the central math relation or operation in a problem statement—things like the preposition “of” in “probability of …,” the participle “picked” in “letters picked without replacement,” the noun “denominator” in “common denominator of …,” or even the Japanese conjunction および (“and”). In other words, it flags the functional token that ties the mathematical operator to its arguments.
