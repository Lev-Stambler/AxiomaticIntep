Feature Explanation

This neuron is basically a “word-start” or “segment-start” detector. It spikes on the very first subword or token of a new element—whether that’s a month in a date, the first letter of a title or acronym, a numeric bound in an expression, or the initial BPE piece of a word (across English, French, Japanese, etc.). In short, it marks the beginnings of structural units (headings, list items, date fields, word prefixes) rather than content further inside.
