Feature Explanation

This feature is essentially a detector for the subword token “w” when it appears as the first letter of a word (i.e. the “w-” prefix in words like “worn,” “woken,” “wistful,” “winnable,” “wares,” “wamy,” “welling,” etc.). It lights up whenever the model encounters that isolated “w” subtoken at the start of a full word.
