Feature Explanation

This feature isn’t about any single topic or domain word, but about “break‐points” in the text—i.e. the spots where one unit ends and another begins. It lights up on prepositions or function words that introduce PPs (of, de, within), on the very first subword of longer content‐words (exc- in “excision,” emic in “anemic,” prob in “probability,” calculat- in “calculated,” etc.), on punctuation that delimits phrases (hyphens, quotes), and even on script or domain‐shift tokens (www, Cyrillic letters, Assyrian). In effect it is a general boundary/segmentation detector marking the edges of morphemes, words, phrases or language‐style shifts.
