Feature Explanation

This feature fires on the little “label” characters that introduce structured markers in technical/academic text—chiefly the single‐letter prefixes in bracketed citations (e.g. the “b” in [@b6], “R” in (R5–8), etc.) and analogous single‐letter tokens in section IDs or HTML/Markdown tags (e.g. `<a>`, `<li>`, code fences). It thus abstracts the signalling of bibliographic or structural markers in scientific and technical documents.
