Feature Explanation

This feature picks out “markup” or “structural” tokens that mark boundaries in richly formatted text—things like the single‐letter citation prefixes (b/B/R in [@b3], [@B5], [@R2] etc.), HTML/XML tags (li, a, code), and LaTeX delimiter commands (\left, \right). It signals tokens that aren’t content words but serve to open, close or identify structural/formatting elements in academic, code or math contexts.
