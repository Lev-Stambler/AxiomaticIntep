Feature Explanation

This neuron fires on the little “glue” words and symbols that link names to their provenance or category membership—tokens like “from,” “of,” “an”/“and,” “&,” etc., as they appear in proper-noun phrases (People from X, Hall of Fame, B&N, S&W, New York Post, airport or place names, scientific site names, etc.). It’s essentially a relational/connective-phrase detector in entity‐description contexts.
